## Hi, I'm hair-0

I am a Mechanical Engineering undergraduate applying for a Computer Science master's, focusing on embodied intelligence, VLA and VLM.

My undergraduate training is in mechanical engineering: mechanics, control, mechatronics and hands-on engineering practice. Through coursework and projects I gradually moved toward intelligent systems — especially making models step out of the screen to perceive, reason and act in the physical world. This motivated me to self-study machine learning, computer vision and robot learning, and to apply for a CS master's.

## Research Interests

- **Embodied AI**: perception, planning and control of robots in the real world
- **Vision-Language-Action (VLA)**: grounding visual and language instructions into robot actions
- **Vision-Language Models (VLM)**: multimodal understanding, visual grounding and reasoning
- **Robot Learning**: imitation learning, reinforcement learning and Sim2Real transfer
